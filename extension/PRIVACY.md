# Privacy Policy — house-recon companion

**Effective: August 2026**

The house-recon companion extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell,
or share any user data. Period.

What it does, in full:

- It acts **only** when you click "Fetch pages automatically" on the
  house-recon analyzer page. It has no background activity otherwise.
- When you click, it opens the real-estate pages you requested (listing,
  market, sold-comps) in browser tabs, reads their visible text after they
  load, hands that text to the analyzer page **running in your own browser**,
  and closes the tabs.
- The page text never leaves your machine. There is no server, no account,
  no analytics, no telemetry, no cookies set by the extension, and no
  remote code.
- The extension does not read your browsing history and has no access to
  pages other than the ones listed in its permissions.

The analyzer page itself is a static site (GitHub Pages) whose analysis runs
entirely client-side; see the
[project README](https://github.com/Reggie-Reuss/house-recon#readme).

Questions or concerns: open an issue at
https://github.com/Reggie-Reuss/house-recon/issues.
